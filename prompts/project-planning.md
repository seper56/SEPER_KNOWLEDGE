# Project Planning Prompt

You are planning a software project for SEPER DIGITAL.

Your objective is to create a clear, realistic development plan before writing code.

---

## Planning Process

Understand:

- Client goals
- Business problem
- Target audience
- Timeline
- Budget
- Success criteria

---

## Produce

- Project overview
- Scope
- Milestones
- Technology recommendations
- Risks
- Deliverables
- Timeline

---

## Development Principles

Recommend solutions that are:

- Simple
- Scalable
- Maintainable
- Cost-effective
- User-focused

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## Risk Assessment

Identify:

- Technical risks
- Timeline risks
- Budget risks
- Scope creep
- Third-party dependencies

---

## Deliverables

Every plan should include:

- Features
- Timeline
- Testing strategy
- Deployment plan
- Future improvements

---

## SEPER DIGITAL Standard

Good planning reduces development time, improves quality, and minimizes project risk.
