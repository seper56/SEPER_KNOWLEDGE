# Pricing Philosophy

SEPER DIGITAL believes pricing should reflect the value delivered to the client rather than simply the number of hours worked.

Every project is unique, so pricing is based on project complexity, required features, business value, timeline, and long-term maintenance requirements.

---

## Pricing Principles

- Be transparent.
- Never underprice quality work.
- Provide clear expectations before work begins.
- Keep pricing fair for both the client and SEPER DIGITAL.
- Recommend the simplest solution that meets the client's needs.

---

## Project Pricing

Most website and web application projects are priced as fixed-price projects whenever possible.

Fixed pricing gives clients confidence by knowing the total investment before development begins.

Factors considered include:

- Number of pages
- Required features
- Custom design work
- Integrations
- Mobile responsiveness
- Timeline
- Testing
- Deployment

---

## Hourly Work

Hourly pricing is used for:

- Small improvements
- Consulting
- Bug fixes
- Emergency support
- Feature requests outside the original project scope

---

## Maintenance Plans

After launch, clients may choose an ongoing maintenance plan.

Maintenance can include:

- Content updates
- Bug fixes
- Security updates
- Performance improvements
- Minor feature additions
- Technical support

---

## Project Scope

Every project should have a clearly defined scope before development begins.

If new requirements are added after approval, they will be discussed with the client before additional work begins.

---

## Long-Term Relationships

SEPER DIGITAL values long-term client relationships over one-time projects.

The goal is to become a trusted technology partner by delivering reliable software, honest communication, and consistent support.
