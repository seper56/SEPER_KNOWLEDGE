# Client Process

## Philosophy

Clients should always know what to expect.

Communication should be honest, professional, and transparent from the first conversation to long-term support.

---

# Step 1

Lead contacts SEPER DIGITAL.

Possible sources:

- Referral
- Website
- Social Media
- Marketplace
- Email

---

# Step 2

Discovery Call

Understand:

- Business
- Goals
- Audience
- Timeline
- Budget
- Required features

Never assume requirements.

Ask questions.

---

# Step 3

Proposal

Client receives:

- Scope
- Timeline
- Pricing
- Deliverables
- Payment expectations

---

# Step 4

Project Begins

Development starts after:

- Scope approval
- Initial payment (if required)
- Requirements confirmed

---

# Step 5

Progress Updates

Clients receive regular updates.

Examples:

- Milestones completed
- Screenshots
- Demonstrations
- Feature previews

---

# Step 6

Client Review

Collect feedback.

Implement approved revisions.

---

# Step 7

Launch

Deliver:

- Final product
- Documentation
- Training (if required)
- Deployment confirmation

---

# Step 8

Long-Term Support

Offer:

- Maintenance
- Improvements
- Additional features
- Future projects

---

# Communication Standards

Always:

- Respond professionally.
- Set realistic expectations.
- Communicate delays early.
- Document important decisions.
- Respect the client's time.

Trust is built through communication.
